# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and give constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as when someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

To report conduct privately, use the [Acceptora contact page](https://www.acceptora.com/contact) to request a confidential communication channel. Do not include credentials, personal data, vulnerability details, or private source material in the initial request.
